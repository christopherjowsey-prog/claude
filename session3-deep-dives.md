### Common Patterns Among Winners

1. **Hyper-niche focus** — solve one specific expensive headache
2. **Build from own pain** — DocuSketch (insurance), ScreenshotAPI (freelancer needs)
3. **Community-first launch** — Indie Hackers, Reddit, "build in public"
4. **Portfolio approach** — some founders run 3-4 tiny products stacked together
5. **One-week cycles** — 1 week build, 1 week market (Bannerbear model)
6. **Near-zero CAC** — content SEO + community + build in public

### The Golden Stack ($0 to Start)

- **Next.js** — framework (free)
- **Supabase** — database + auth (free tier generous)
- **Stripe** — payments (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction)
- **Vercel** — hosting (free tier)
- **Tailwind + Shadcn** — UI (free)
- First paid upgrade at ~60-100 customers (Supabase Pro $25/mo)
- Total: **$0/month to start**, under $50/month to $100K ARR

### Common Failure Modes

1. **Building something nobody wants** — #1 killer. Validate before building.
2. **No distribution plan** — "Build it and they'll come" doesn't work
3. **Too broad** — generic tools compete with funded companies
4. **Pricing too low** — $5/month products need massive volume
5. **Giving up too early** — most quit before month 6

## Part 2: Retirement Living Lead Gen — The Opportunity

### The Australian Market

- **~2,200+ retirement villages** across Australia
- **200,000+ residents** currently living in villages
- **Market size:** $6.03 billion (2025), projected **8.17% CAGR to 2033**
- **Penetration:** Less than 10% of 65+ population in villages (huge upside)
- **Occupancy:** 90% nationally (high demand)
- **Fragmentation:** Many operators run just ONE village — they need help marketing
- **Development pipeline:** 10,500+ new units planned in next 3 years

But the long tail = **hundreds of small operators with 1-5 villages** who have minimal marketing capability.

### The Current Players

#### Downsizing.com.au
- **Australia's leading over-50s retirement property portal**
- **2 million+ visitors per year**
- **250+ paying corporate advertisers**
- Business model: listing fees + advertising packages
- Part of DOCOMEMONDAY Village & Care Media group (family business)
- Content-heavy: news, guides, calculators
- **Weakness:** directory model — search by location, filter by type. No personalised matching.

#### Villages.com.au
- **5,000+ properties listed**
- Member of DOCOMEMONDAY Village & Care Media group (same parent as Downsizing)
- 10+ years in market, visited hundreds of communities
- Surveyed 15,000+ residents
- **Ad model:** Australia divided into 81 lifestyle regions, max 4 banners per region
- Pricing: not public, contact sales
- **Weakness:** banner ad model = old school. Operators pay for exposure, not results.

#### Other Players
- **Compare Villages** — "Australia's only retirement living comparison website"
- **Compare Communities** — 20 years in the industry
- **Retirement Living Online** — directory style
- **SimplyRetirement.com.au** — information portal

### What the Big Publishers Get Wrong

1. **Directory model** — search → browse → enquire. No intelligence, no matching.
2. **Operator pays for visibility, not results** — banner ads and listing fees regardless of leads delivered
3. **No personalisation** — every visitor sees the same listings. No lifestyle/budget/care-need matching.
4. **Content is generic** — "top 10 retirement villages in Sydney" listicles. Nothing hyper-local or community-specific.
5. **The buyer journey is broken** — families spend 12-18 months researching. No platform nurtures that journey.
6. **No data feedback loop** — operators don't know which content/features drive actual move-ins
7. **Both major sites are owned by the same group** (DOCOMEMONDAY) — market is essentially a duopoly with no real innovation pressure

### The US Model: What Works Overseas

#### A Place for Mom (APFM)
- **Largest senior living referral service in North America**
- **15,000+ communities** in network
- **Revenue model:** charges communities **100-150% of first month's rent** per successful placement
- Median assisted living rent: **$5,350/month** → referral fee of $5,350-$8,025 per move-in
- **Revenue growth:** 30% in 2021, 20%+ in 2022
- **$175M raised** in 2022 at **$1B+ valuation**
- **$100M+ annual marketing spend**
- Free for families — communities pay on successful placement
- Controls **36% of senior living referral websites**
- Combined with Caring.com = **60% of all senior living lead gen sites**

**APFM's problems (= your opportunity):**
- Only recommends facilities in their paying network (biased)
- 40% of referrals pay above family's budget (upselling)
- Excludes Medicaid recipients
- 1/3 of recommended facilities cited for poor care
- Under US Senate investigation for deceptive practices

#### Seniorly
- Tech-forward alternative to APFM
- NIH-funded startup **Olera** also entering — subscription model where providers access prospect profiles

### What Nobody in Australia Is Doing

**The gap between villages.com.au/downsizing.com.au and A Place for Mom is massive.** Australian market has:
- No performance-based lead gen (pay-per-move-in)
- No AI-powered matching
- No CRM/nurture journey for the 12-18 month decision cycle
- No data-driven recommendation engine
- No family/decision-maker tools (adult children often drive the decision)

## Part 3: Your Differentiated Angle

### Your Moat: The Full Database

Having the complete database of retirement living firms is a **genuine competitive advantage** that took the big publishers years to build. Here are angles that exploit this:

### Angle 1: "The Comparison Engine" (Micro-SaaS Play)
**What:** Build a data-rich comparison tool that goes deeper than any existing directory.
- Detailed cost breakdowns (entry fees, ongoing fees, exit fees, deferred management fees)
- Resident satisfaction data (aggregate reviews, NPS)
- Amenity-by-amenity comparison
- Financial modelling (total cost of living over 5/10/15 years)
- Waitlist status and availability
- **Why it's different:** Villages.com.au shows listings. You show *intelligence*.
- **Revenue:** Freemium for consumers, operators pay for enhanced profiles + leads
- **Startup cost:** $0-50 (golden stack)

### Angle 2: "The Matchmaker" (Lead Gen Play)
**What:** AI-powered matching platform — think "Domain meets Tinder for retirement living"
- Families fill out a detailed profile: budget, location, lifestyle preferences, care needs, social preferences, pet-friendliness, etc.
- Algorithm matches to top 5-10 villages from your database
- Warm, qualified leads delivered to operators
- **Revenue:** Pay-per-qualified-lead ($50-200/lead) or pay-per-tour ($100-300)
- **Why it's different:** Operators pay for results, not visibility. Leads are pre-qualified.
- **Startup cost:** $0-50

### Angle 3: "The Family Decision Hub" (Content + Lead Gen)
**What:** Content platform targeting adult children (the real decision-makers)
- "How to talk to mum about downsizing" guides
- Cost calculators and financial planning tools
- Comparison tools
- Community reviews from families
- **Revenue:** Affiliate/lead gen to operators + financial planning partnerships
- **Why it's different:** Nobody targets the adult child. Everyone targets the retiree.
- **Startup cost:** $0-30

### Angle 4: "The APFM Model, Localised" (High-Value Lead Gen)
**What:** Australian version of A Place for Mom — free advisory service for families.
- Free phone/video consultations with families
- Match to suitable villages from your database
- Charge operators a referral fee on successful placement
- **Revenue:** Referral fee = 1 month's rent equivalent ($2,000-$5,000+ per placement)
- At just 5 placements/month = $10K-25K/month
- **Why it's different:** Nobody does this in Australia. The DOCOMEMONDAY duopoly is passive (directory). This is active (advisory).
- **Startup cost:** $0-100 (phone + website)

### Angle 5: "Operator Intelligence Dashboard" (B2B SaaS)
**What:** Analytics and market intelligence tool for retirement village operators
- Local market benchmarking (your pricing vs competitors)
- Demand heatmaps (where are people searching?)
- Occupancy trend data
- Lead quality scoring
- Competitive intelligence
- **Revenue:** $200-500/month per operator subscription
- 50 operators × $300/month = $15K/month
- **Why it's different:** No one gives operators data about their market. They're flying blind.
- **Startup cost:** $0-50

## Recommended Path: Combine Angles 2 + 4

**Phase 1 (Month 1-2):** Build the matching engine (Angle 2)
- Simple quiz/questionnaire for families
- Algorithm matches against your database
- Deliver leads to operators via email
- Charge $0 initially — prove the model

**Phase 2 (Month 2-4):** Add advisory layer (Angle 4)
- Personal consultations for complex cases
- Build relationships with operators
- Negotiate referral fees ($50-200/qualified lead, or % of first month)
- Test pay-per-tour model

**Phase 3 (Month 4-8):** Scale with content (Angle 3)
- SEO content targeting "retirement villages [suburb]" long-tail
- Target adult children decision-makers
- Build email nurture sequences for the 12-18 month journey

**Phase 4 (Month 8-12):** Add operator tools (Angle 5)
- Market intelligence dashboard
- Recurring SaaS revenue layer on top of lead gen
