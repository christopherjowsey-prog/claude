# $100 to $10,000 — Autonomous Research Log

## Objective
Research and validate realistic strategies to turn $100 into $10,000 with minimal ongoing input. Each session builds on previous findings.

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Feasibility**: Can this realistically be done starting with $100?
- **Autonomy**: How much can be automated or done with AI assistance?
- **Time to ROI**: How quickly could returns start?
- **Risk Level**: What's the downside?
- **Scalability**: Can early profits compound into $10k?

#### Ideas Discovered (8 total)

**1. Niche Community Monetization**
- Build paid resource hubs for underserved communities
- Startup: $20-50 | Revenue: Paid listings $50-200/mo, sponsorships $200-500/mo
- Evidence: OpenAlternative grew $0 to $57K revenue (516% YoY), hit $6K MRR by Dec 2025
- Timeline: 8-12 months to $10K

**2. Data Products**
- Curate, clean, and sell niche datasets
- Startup: $5-30 | Revenue: $49-299/dataset, $29-99/mo subscriptions
- Evidence: AI side hustlers earning $2K-6K/month combining AI + domain expertise
- Timeline: 4-8 months to $10K

**3. Automation-as-a-Service (n8n/Make.com)**
- Build custom workflows for small businesses
- Startup: $7-15/mo | Revenue: Setup $200-700 + retainers $1,200-1,700/mo
- Evidence: Freelancer documented $8,200/month in 7 months from Reddit comments alone
- Timeline: 3-5 months to $10K

**4. Digital Real Estate (Domains + Niche Sites)**
- Buy trending domains, build thin affiliate sites, flip at 30-45x monthly profit
- Startup: $60-100 | Revenue: Domain flips $200-5,000, site sales $5K-13.5K
- Evidence: Content websites selling at 30-45x monthly profits in 2026
- Timeline: 6-12 months to $10K

**5. Event/Experience Arbitrage**
- Organize paid virtual workshops with free expert speakers
- Startup: $13-30 | Revenue: $30-50/ticket × 30-50 attendees = $900-2,500/event
- Evidence: Stagetimer grew from weekend experiment to $20K/month
- Timeline: 6-10 months to $10K

**6. AI Agent Services**
- Build and deploy AI agents for business tasks
- Startup: $20-50 | Revenue: Setup $500-2,000 + retainers $200-500/mo
- Evidence: AI agents market $8B→$11.7B (2025→2026). 70%+ operating margins reported.
- Timeline: 3-5 months to $10K

**7. Affiliate/Referral Stacking**
- Layer recurring-commission affiliate programs via SEO workflow content
- Startup: $20-60 | Revenue: 20-40% recurring commissions per referral
- Evidence: Reddit marketer grew $267/month to $21,853/month over 19 months
- Timeline: 8-14 months to $10K

**8. Skill Stacking**
- Combine free certifications into premium cross-functional consulting
- Startup: $0-100 | Revenue: Consulting $100-250/hr, retainers $2K-5K/mo
- Evidence: 64% of certified professionals got 3-20% pay raise within a year
- Timeline: 4-8 months to $10K

#### Recommended Combos from Research
- **Fastest:** AI Agent Services (#6) + Automation-as-a-Service (#3) — overlapping tools/clients
- **Most Passive:** Affiliate Stacking (#7) + Niche Community (#1) — compounds over time
- **Lowest Risk:** Skill Stacking (#8) + Event Arbitrage (#5) — zero startup cost
